Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Welcome to AAYA AI Assistant ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal data and permissions are handled when using our Android application and services. We are firmly committed to user privacy, offline-first processing, and transparency.

1. Overview & Privacy Principles

  • No Selling of Data: We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or contact details to third-party ad networks or brokers.
  • Offline-First Speed: Core hardware controls (flashlight, volume, brightness, offline alarms, timetable recall) are processed directly on your Android device without cloud transmission.
  • Guest Mode Bypass: Users can use AAYA in "Guest Mode" completely offline without signing in or providing any personal identity.

2. Permissions Explained (Why We Request Them)

In full compliance with Google Play Developer Policy, below is a complete and transparent breakdown of every runtime permission requested by AAYA:

🎙️ Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE)

Purpose: Required for real-time speech recognition when you tap the mic or speak voice commands. Audio is converted to text either through on-device speech recognition or transient Google Speech APIs. We do not store, archive, or record ambient audio in the background without user initiation.

📖 Contacts (READ_CONTACTS)

Purpose: Enables voice commands like "Call Papa" or "Send SMS to Rahul". Contacts are matched locally on your device using bilingual phonetics. Your full contact book is never uploaded or scraped.

💬 SMS & Scheduled Messaging (SEND_SMS)

Purpose: Allows the hands-free transmission of text messages and scheduled birthday/reminder SMS that you explicitly ask AAYA to send. SMS dispatch occurs via standard device telephony providers.

📍 Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

Purpose: Used exclusively when you ask for your current postal PIN code, local weather, district info, or route navigation. Location coordinates are queried live and never tracked on external servers.

📞 Call Phone & Call Logs (CALL_PHONE & READ_CALL_LOG)

Purpose: Allows hands-free telephone dialing and queries such as "Aakhiri call kiski thi?" (Who called last?). Call logs are read strictly to fulfill user-initiated verbal questions.

🫧 Floating Overlay (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

Purpose: Displays the Siri-style animated waveform card over existing applications so you can interact with AAYA without exiting your current app.

3. Cloud Services & Third-Party Integration

  • Google Firebase Authentication: If you choose Google Sign-In, Firebase securely verifies your Google account UID, email, and display name.
  • Google Cloud Firestore: Used to securely maintain your user profile, daily query quota, and optional cloud sync across devices.
  • Google Gemini AI API: When handling complex generative queries, text prompts are transmitted over encrypted TLS (HTTPS) connections to Google Gemini models to generate natural language answers.

4. Data Retention & User Rights

You have full ownership of your data:

  • You can clear all stored memories and local notes at any time from within the app settings.
  • To request full account deletion and removal of your Firebase profile, simply contact us at the email below, and your record will be purged within 48 hours.
